# Privacy Policy

- **App:** Daykit (Windows app with widgets for the Windows 11 Widgets Board)
- **Developer:** Appfine
- **Contact:** [appfine@outlook.com](mailto:appfine@outlook.com)
- **Effective date:** November 26, 2026

This policy explains what information Daykit ("the app") handles and what happens to it. In short: **the app does not collect, send or share any personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device.**

## 1. Information the app stores

The app stores only the content you create in it, so that it can show it to you in the app window and in its widgets:

- **To-do:** task titles, whether each task is done, and when it was created.
- **Habits:** habit names, the days of the week each habit is planned for, the days you marked as done, and when each habit was created.
- **Pomodoro:** timer settings and the state of the current timer and sessions.
- **Notes:** the text of your notes.
- **Trackers:** the names and values of the trackers you set up.
- **App settings:** your preferences, such as the app theme and whether and when the daily habits reminder is shown.
- **Error log:** if something goes wrong, technical details of the error (the time and the place in the app's code where it happened). It does not include your content, and it stays on your device like everything else.

Some of these modules may not be available in every version of the app. The app does not ask for your name, e-mail address, location, contacts, or any other personal identifiers, and it does not access your camera, microphone, files or other apps.

## 2. Where it is stored

Your content is saved in files on your own device, inside the app's private data folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\<app package>\LocalState`). Only your Windows user account and the app itself can normally access this folder.

The app has no accounts, no cloud sync and no servers. It does not connect to the internet to send or receive your content.

## 3. What the app does not do

- It does **not** send your content or any information about you to the developer or to anyone else.
- It does **not** include analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking code.
- It does **not** sell, rent or share any information with third parties.

## 4. Windows and the Widgets Board

To display a widget, the app passes the widget's content (for example, your open tasks or a note) to the Windows Widgets Board, which is part of Windows, on the same device. How Windows and the Microsoft Store handle data, including diagnostic data and crash reports that Microsoft may collect and share with developers in anonymous, aggregated form, is described in the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). The developer has no control over that processing.

## 5. Habit reminders

If the daily habits reminder is on, the app asks Windows on your device to show a notification at the time you chose, listing the habits planned for that day that are not marked yet. The notification is scheduled and shown locally by Windows; nothing is sent off your device. You can turn the reminder off in the app's settings or block the app's notifications in Windows **Settings → System → Notifications**.

## 6. Backups

If you use Windows backup or a similar tool, it may copy the app's data folder together with your other files. That is controlled by your Windows settings, not by the app.

## 7. How to delete your data

- Delete individual items (tasks, habits, notes, trackers) inside the app at any time.
- To delete all of the app's data at once, either uninstall the app, or open **Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Daykit → Advanced options → Reset**. Both remove the app's data folder from your device.

Because the developer never receives your data, there is nothing stored on the developer's side to delete.

## 8. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds a feature that sends data off your device (for example, sync between devices), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and any such feature will be off until you explicitly turn it on. The effective date at the top of this page shows when the policy last changed.

## 10. Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data: [appfine@outlook.com](mailto:appfine@outlook.com).
